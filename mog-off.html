How it works

Two players appear side by side and receive facial geometry game scores. The scores come from facial landmark geometry, not random numbers. Overall rating is out of 10; in a two-player match, the higher score wins.

For safety, the game analyzes facial landmarks locally without storing your camera feed. No microphone permission is requested.

Settings

Camera permission is requested only when you press Start Camera.
